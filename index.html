HAILMARY
Probabilistic Fantasy Football Optimizer · CS109

Most fantasy apps give you a single projected score. HailMary gives you a full probability distribution.

📊MLE Projections — Player μ and σ fitted on actual weekly data, recalculated every week
🎯Win Probability — Uses the Central Limit Theorem to model your full lineup score as a normal distribution
🔥Boom % — Logistic regression model predicting standout weeks, trained on 2025 season data
⚡Battle Mode — Head-to-head score differential distribution with P(you win) calculated analytically

Set your lineup → set your opponent's lineup → go to ⚡ Battle to see your win probability.
